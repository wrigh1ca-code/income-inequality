Two Things Can Be True

Things got better for everyone. And the gap got wider.

Four ways to measure well-being in America. For each, the left column shows how lives improved; the right shows how the distance between the top and the bottom grew.

Measure 1

Market income

Captures: what the economy pays households before government. Misses: taxes, transfers, health benefits, household size.

Measure 2

Income after taxes and transfers

Captures: market income plus Social Security, Medicaid, SNAP and refundable credits, minus federal taxes. Misses: saving and borrowing, state and local taxes.

Things got better for everyone
And the gap got wider
Measure 3

Consumption and quality of life

Captures: what households actually spend, own and use — and how long they live to enjoy it. Misses: survey under-reporting, quality change, leisure.

Things got better for everyone
And the gap got wider
Measure 4

Wealth

Captures: home equity, retirement accounts, stocks and businesses, minus debt. Misses: Social Security and pension promises; very sensitive to asset prices.
